## English (en)

### Short description
```
Shop plants and garden goods, track orders and your Smart Buy savings scheme.
```

### Full description
```
Natural Plantation brings our shop and our Smart Buy savings scheme to your phone.

SHOP
- Browse plants, seeds, groceries and garden tools by category, or search by name
- See clear prices with tax included, and choose sizes or weights where they apply
- Add to your cart even when your signal is weak; place the order when you are online
- Pay cash on delivery, or by bank transfer with the details shown at checkout
- Follow every order from "received" to "delivered" and get a notification at each step
- Save delivery addresses and favourite items

SMART BUY MEMBERS
- See your scheme, your payments and the draws in one place
- Check your statement any time, even offline

FIELD OFFICERS
- See your assigned deliveries and complete them with the customer's delivery code
- See your members and your earnings

FOR EVERYONE
- English, Tamil and Sinhala
- Light and fast, works on low-end phones
- Your data is protected, and you can delete your account in the app whenever you like

Customers can register with a mobile number. Smart Buy members and field officers receive their first login from their branch. Need help? Ask your nearest Natural Plantation branch.
```

## Other store fields
- **Category:** Shopping. **Tags:** Shopping, Lifestyle.
- **Contact email / phone / website:** the Support Email, Support Phone and Website entered in POS Settings > Company Information (shown by `GET /api/app/v1/company`).
- **Privacy policy URL:** `privacy_url` from `GET /api/app/v1/company` (`<API base>/legal/privacy?c=<slug>`). **Account deletion URL:** `delete_account_url` from the same call (`/legal/delete-account?c=<slug>`). Both pages are generated from POS company settings, so fill Legal Name, Address, Support Email and Support Phone there first (the endpoint's `missing` list shows what is still empty). The old static HTML files with placeholders were removed.
