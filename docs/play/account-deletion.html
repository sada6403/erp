Delete your Natural Plantation account

App: Natural Plantation (Android) · Operator: [COMPANY LEGAL NAME]

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Read the warning, type your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out on all devices.

Option 2: ask us

If you cannot sign in or no longer have the app, send a request from the phone number registered with your account:

Email: [SUPPORT EMAIL]
Phone / WhatsApp: [SUPPORT PHONE]
Subject: Delete my Natural Plantation account
Include: your name and the mobile number or member number on the account.

We may ask a question to confirm it is really you. We complete verified requests within [7] days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and password (the account becomes "Deleted user" and cannot be used to sign in)
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Wish list
  • In-app notifications
  • Notification tokens for your devices

What we keep, and why

Order, payment, delivery and Smart Buy savings records are part of our business accounts. We keep them for as long as accounting and tax rules require. After deletion they are no longer connected to a login, and the app cannot show them to anyone. They are not used for marketing.

Deleting only some data

To remove only certain data (for example a saved address), do it in the app under Settings, or contact us at the address above.
