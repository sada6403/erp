2. How we use it

To run the shop and Smart Buy services, deliver orders, send notifications about your orders and account, give customer support, prevent fraud and keep our accounts as the law requires. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

  • Our staff, only as needed for their job (for example a branch sees the orders for its customers).
  • Service providers that run the app on our behalf: our hosting server, and the push notification services of Expo and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), which receive your device's notification token and the notification text so they can deliver it.
  • WhatsApp: our staff may send your first login details to you through WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

4. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access and keep the server up to date.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account while you use the app. Order and accounting records are kept for as long as our legal, tax and accounting duties require, even after you delete your account (see section 6). Notification tokens are removed when you sign out or delete your account.

6. Your choices: access, correction, deletion

You can change your name, addresses, language and notification settings in the app. To delete your account, open Settings → Delete account in the app, or follow the steps at [ACCOUNT DELETION PAGE URL]. When your account is deleted we remove your name, phone number, email and password, saved addresses, wish list, notifications and device tokens, and you can no longer sign in. Order, payment and Smart Buy records that we must keep for accounting stay in our books without your app login. You may also ask us to correct or delete your data, or tell us about a concern, by contacting us above. You have rights under the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka, where it applies.

7. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
