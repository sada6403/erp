# POS ERP 2.11.2

## POS billing
- **Scanning:** a scanned barcode is added to the cart at once. A trailing scanner
  Enter does not add it a second time. Slow manual typing still needs Enter.
- **Weighed items:** while the quantity panel is open, every key goes to the
  quantity. A barcode scanned by mistake is rejected instead of being added as a
  quantity.
- **Sidebar:** hidden on the billing screen for full-width billing. The menu
  button shows it again.

## Bills
- **Cancelling bills:** select one or several bills, give a reason and cancel
  them. Only Admins and managers can do this.
- **Effect of a cancel:** stock, batches, credit, coupons and the SmartBuy wallet
  are reversed. Cancelled bills are left out of sales totals. The change is
  audited and synced to the cloud.

## Keyboard stock entry (supermarket)
- **Entry order:** product, quantity (`12`, `2 ctn`, `500g`), free quantity,
  purchase price, supplier offer (`10%`, `150`, `10+1`), selling price, MRP,
  customer offer (`90`, `10%`, `-20`) with dates, barcode, batch, MFD and expiry.
- **Shortcuts:** Enter moves to the next field. F2 search, F3 edit a line, F4 new
  product, F6 offer, F7 barcode, F8 add line, F9 save draft. Ctrl+Up/Down selects
  a line, Ctrl+Del removes it and Ctrl+Z undoes the last line. Ctrl+Enter opens
  the review, and Enter confirms it.
- **Comparison panel:** previous and latest purchase price, current selling
  price, stock now and after the receipt, effective cost after offers, weighted
  average cost, suggested price and margin.
- **Barcodes:** additional barcodes per product (synced), internal barcode
  generation (Alt+G) and label printing. POS scanning finds additional barcodes.
- **Customer offers:** saved as dated product discounts after the receipt is
  saved.
- **Drafts:** the receipt is saved automatically and offered for restore after a
  crash.

## Supplier quotation and reorder
- **Reorder list:** select a supplier (F2) to load only that supplier's products.
  Each row shows stock, minimum and maximum levels, incoming orders, 30-day
  sales, last price and a recommended quantity.
- **Editing the list:** arrow keys, Space, Delete, Enter/F3 for the quantity, and
  F4 to add a product or a new-item request. A product with no supplier can be
  linked to this supplier after you confirm (Y/N).
- **Duplicate prevention:** a product already on a pending order is shown with
  its incoming quantity and needs a manager override.
- **Sending the request:** F9 saves the quotation, Ctrl+P prints it (or saves it
  as a PDF), and finalizing offers to send it on WhatsApp. The supplier document
  contains no costs, selling prices or margins.
- **Supplier prices:** "Record prices" stores the prices, discounts and free
  quantities the supplier confirms. Only then can the quotation be converted into
  a purchase order.
- **Stock:** stock increases only when the goods are received.

## Schema and sync
All changes are additive:
- `product_barcodes` table (desktop and cloud, synced).
- `products.max_stock_level`.
- Quotation columns on `purchase_orders` and `purchase_items`.

The cloud adds these automatically through `ensureBatchSyncSchema`. The backend
is deployed before the desktop update.
