# POS ERP 2.11.1

## POS billing

Loose items (kg, g, l, ml, m) no longer open a centre popup. A quantity panel is
docked at the top of the cart sidebar, so the bill stays visible:

- Enter adds, Esc cancels. Units are converted (for example `750g` on a kg product
  adds 0.75 kg).
- Alt+1 to Alt+4 add quick amounts (250g, 500g, 1kg, 2kg, or the ml/l and cm/m
  equivalents).
- The line total updates while typing.
- F3 or Ctrl+Enter edits the selected cart line.

## Purchasing and stock (supermarket mode)

- Purchase entry: search products by name, barcode or SKU, and create products
  inline, linked to the supplier. Enter free quantity, line discount, MRP and the
  entry unit, with unit conversion.
- Orders and receiving: draft orders, direct receipts and partial receiving. Stale
  retries never add stock twice.
- Batch details on arrival: batch number, manufacture date and expiry date are
  entered per line when goods arrive. Orders do not ask for them. Expiry-tracked
  products cannot be received without an expiry date, and the expiry date must be
  after the manufacture date.
- Batch costs and prices: each receipt keeps its own batch with its cost, selling
  price, MRP and expiry. Old batch prices are preserved.
- Selling from batches: FEFO/FIFO allocation never sells expired batches, and the
  selling price cannot exceed MRP.
- Pricing during entry: previous and latest purchase price, stock after receipt,
  weighted average cost and suggested price are shown.
- Pricing policy: batch price, latest price or weighted average + markup. Manual
  price changes need a reason and are recorded in the audit log.
- Supplier returns and balances: supplier returns, and supplier due and credit
  balances.
- Reports: expired, near-expiry and low-stock alerts, plus stock movement and
  batch reports.
- Bulk import: stock-in import includes supplier, free quantity, discount, MRP,
  batch and dates, with preview, validation and an all-or-nothing import.

## Other

- Optional POS Testing Mode uses a separate sandbox copy of the database and blocks
  external services. If the sandbox cannot be read, the app shows an error and
  quits instead of opening live data.
- Payment dialog shortcuts: Alt+A agent, Alt+G coupon, Alt+M amount.

## Sync and schema

All changes are additive. The cloud MySQL tenant schema adds `purchase_returns`,
`purchase_return_items` and the new purchase/batch/supplier columns automatically
through `ensureBatchSyncSchema`. Deploy the backend before desktops upgrade, so
that purchase return sync is accepted. `backend/database/supermarket-inventory.sql`
is for an optional branch PostgreSQL database only, and it is not applied
automatically.
