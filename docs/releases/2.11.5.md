# POS ERP 2.11.5

Desktop internal updater release, 2026-11-27. Includes the 2.11.4 keyboard billing and same-bill extra-item changes, plus the product-card stray-zero correction.

- Wider billing cart, keyboard-accessible quantity/price/discount fields, visible payment actions, and wrapping toolbars/shortcut hints.
- Ctrl+B recalls a bill by number; Ctrl+P reprints it. Extra products can be appended to a completed, paid retail bill with a separate cash/card/bank payment while preserving its number and original lines/payments.
- Extra-item transactions validate stock, prices and discounts, record batch/commission/audit changes, and reject stale retries. Locked bills, active returns and online-order bills are excluded.
- Product cards no longer render a numeric zero when variant_count is zero. Actual product prices and stock quantities are unchanged.
