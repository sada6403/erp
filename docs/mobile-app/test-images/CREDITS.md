## TEST banners (generated, no third-party material)

They exist only in the staging **test** company (the script refuses any company whose name lacks "test") to exercise banner links (none / flash deals / Smart Buy), ordering and stats. They must never appear in a Play build, screenshot or listing. Remove with `--remove`.
